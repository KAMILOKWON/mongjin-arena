# Content packages

Every publishable story has its own folder and names the experiment that
supports it.

```text
content-NNN-short-name/
├── BRIEF.md               Audience, promise, evidence, and CTA
├── ARTICLE_KO.md          Korean master article
├── ARTICLE_EN.md          English master article
├── SOCIAL.md              Channel-specific derivatives
├── VIDEO.md               Shot list and narration
├── ASSET_LIST.md          Required and completed assets
└── PUBLISH_CHECKLIST.md   Accuracy and release gate
```

Drafts may contain explicit placeholders, but no quantitative claim is
published until the linked experiment is complete and its raw outputs exist.
